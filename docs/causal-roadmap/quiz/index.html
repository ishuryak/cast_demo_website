Self-test · for medical students and residents

Thirty questions on the causal roadmap

These questions follow the causal roadmap primer, from choosing the kind of question to arguing for or against a new randomized trial. Most are short clinical scenarios built on the primer's running example, hypofractionated versus conventional fractionation. After each answer you see why it is right, the most tempting wrong answer explained, and the source. The final report lists your misses and links each one back to the section of the primer that covers it.

Questions and answer letters are shuffled on every start. Keys A to E pick an answer, and Enter submits or moves on.
