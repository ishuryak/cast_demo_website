We connect three existing public projects: causal-roadmap-primer (clinical question, target trial, DAG and assumptions), cast_demo_website (interactive synthetic survival-effect trajectories and runnable R lesson), survcausal-pilot (reproducible benchmarks). These projects use different simulation cohorts; they can share question/target labels and teaching prompts without pretending their results are paired.

Proposed first paper: a roadmap-guided survival analysis workflow for trial-like and, with additional identification assumptions, observational data. Core aims are predictive performance and population-average survival differences over time. Benefit ranking is a bounded, matched simulation extension; a broad clinical HTE workflow is future work. Population CATE is an average conditional on measured X, not a recovered individual causal effect. CAST smooths CSF population-average estimates across time; it does not itself supply patient-specific treatment rankings.

Eight steps: (1) population, treatment strategies, eligibility/time zero and outcome horizon; (2) causal model; (3) observed baseline/treatment/event/censoring data; (4) identification including confounding, treatment support and censoring; (5) statistical estimand after identification; (6) estimator, tuning, sample splitting, uncertainty; (7) assumption-specific sensitivity analyses; (8) results and interpretation. Prediction is a separate empirical task alongside this causal workflow, not proof of identification.

Teaching cycle: ask a question, predict qualitatively, change one simulation mechanism, reveal the known truth, explain the failure and say what cannot be learned from real data alone.
